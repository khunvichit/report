## Palette (as used in the locked template)
- **Primary purple** `#5551FE` — header band, section headings, accents.
- **Soft purple** `#C9C7FF` / `#E6E5FF` — header subtitle, chart bars (secondary).
- **Coral accent** `#F27061` — alerts, "DAILY" badge, current-week bar, reorder border.
- **Warm background** `#F5EDE4`; **card** `#ffffff` (radius 12); **muted card** `#efe7dc`.
- **Text** `#2b2b33` primary, `#8a8a93` muted, `#5b5547` on warm.
- **Status:** green `#1f9d57`/`#1f7a55` (good), red `#d6453a`/`#c43b27` (bad), amber `#b5740a` (watch),
  badge bgs green `#e3f5ec`, amber `#fbeecd`, consign `#e1f5ee`/`#0f6e56`, interco `#eeedfe`/`#5551FE`.

## Type
- **Poppins** (400/500/600/700), fallback Arial/Helvetica. Email clients ignore web-fonts → Arial fallback is expected; do not rely on Poppins rendering in mail.
