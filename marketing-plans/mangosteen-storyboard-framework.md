# Mangosteen Storyboard Framework

## Purpose
This framework helps staff quickly draft short-form video storyboards that match the Mangosteen launch content pillars. It is designed for TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, YouTube Shorts, and in-store screen content.

## Main Campaign Objective
Awareness.

Every storyboard should help more people notice, remember, and become curious about the Mangosteen launch.

## Core Positioning
Mangosteen is a refreshing tropical product inspired by Thailand's Queen of Fruits. It should feel fresh, premium, colorful, and worth trying.

## Key Message
"Discover Thailand's Queen of Fruits in a refreshing new drink."

---

# How Staff Should Use This Framework

## Step 1: Choose One Content Pillar
Pick only one main pillar per video.

1. Product Beauty
2. Tropical Story
3. First Reaction
4. FOMO / Limited-Time Launch
5. In-Store Discovery
6. Staff Recommendation

## Step 2: Choose One Video Format
Pick one simple format.

- 6-second teaser
- 10-second product reveal
- 15-second tasting reaction
- 20-second behind-the-scenes video
- 30-second mini story

## Step 3: Fill in the Storyboard Template
Use the template below.

---

# Universal Storyboard Template

## Video Title
Example: "First Taste of Mangosteen"

## Content Pillar
Example: First Reaction

## Objective
Example: Make customers curious to try the new Mangosteen drink.

## Platform
Example: TikTok / Instagram Reels

## Duration
Example: 15 seconds

## Target Audience
Example: Office workers, young adults, existing customers

## Hook
What will make people stop scrolling in the first 1-2 seconds?

Example:
"Thailand's Queen of Fruits is now a drink."

## Scene-by-Scene Storyboard

| Scene | Time | Visual | Action | Text on Screen | Voice / Sound | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0-2 sec | Close-up of purple drink / mangosteen | Fast reveal | "New Mangosteen is here" | Upbeat music | Grab attention |
| 2 | 3-6 sec | Staff making drink | Pour, shake, serve | "Fresh tropical taste" | Preparation sound | Show product freshness |
| 3 | 7-11 sec | Customer takes first sip | Smile / reaction | "Refreshing? Yes." | Reaction sound | Build social proof |
| 4 | 12-15 sec | Product hero shot | Drink beside fruit | "Try it now" | Music peak | Call to action |

## Call to Action
Example:
"Try the new Mangosteen drink today at participating stores."

## Required Brand Elements
- Mangosteen product name
- Purple visual cue
- Fresh / tropical / refreshing message
- Store or availability mention
- Clear product shot

## Final Checklist
Before posting, confirm:

- Does the video clearly show the product?
- Is the first 2 seconds interesting?
- Does it match only one main content pillar?
- Is the message simple?
- Is there a clear reason to try the product?
- Is the call to action included?
- Is the product visually appetizing?

# Content Pillar Storyboard Guides

## 1. Product Beauty Pillar

### Purpose
Make the product look craveable and premium.

### Best For
- Reels
- TikTok
- Instagram Stories
- Menu screen content

### Story Structure
1. Beautiful close-up
2. Preparation moment
3. Texture / color / ice shot
4. Final hero shot
5. Simple CTA

### Recommended Shots
- Close-up of mangosteen fruit
- Drink being poured
- Ice dropping into cup
- Smooth movement of cup turning
- Staff placing drink on counter
- Final product with purple background

### Hook Examples
- "This purple drink is our newest tropical launch."
- "Meet your new refreshing favorite."
- "Fresh, tropical, and finally here."

### Text-on-Screen Examples
- "New Mangosteen"
- "Fresh tropical taste"
- "Made for refreshing moments"
- "Now available"

### Example Storyboard

| Scene | Time | Visual | Action | Text on Screen | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0-2 sec | Extreme close-up of purple drink | Cup slides into frame | "New Mangosteen" | Stop scroll |
| 2 | 3-5 sec | Ice and drink close-up | Pour over ice | "Refreshing tropical taste" | Show freshness |
| 3 | 6-9 sec | Staff finishing drink | Add lid / garnish | "Made fresh" | Show preparation |
| 4 | 10-13 sec | Hero product shot | Rotate cup slowly | "Thailand's Queen of Fruits" | Build identity |
| 5 | 14-15 sec | Product at counter | Hand reaches for cup | "Try it today" | CTA |

## 2. Tropical Story Pillar

### Purpose
Educate customers and make Mangosteen feel special.

### Best For
- 20-30 second reels
- Facebook posts
- TikTok explainers
- Staff-led content

### Story Structure
1. Introduce Mangosteen
2. Explain why it is special
3. Connect to the drink
4. Show product
5. Invite trial

### Hook Examples
- "Do you know why Mangosteen is called the Queen of Fruits?"
- "Thailand's Queen of Fruits is now a refreshing drink."
- "This tropical fruit inspired our newest launch."

### Text-on-Screen Examples
- "Queen of Fruits"
- "Sweet, tropical, refreshing"
- "Inspired by Thai fruit culture"
- "Now in a new drink"

### Example Storyboard

| Scene | Time | Visual | Action | Text on Screen | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0-3 sec | Mangosteen fruit close-up | Fruit opens | "Queen of Fruits?" | Create curiosity |
| 2 | 4-8 sec | Fruit and drink side by side | Camera pans | "Thailand's tropical favorite" | Educate |
| 3 | 9-14 sec | Staff making drink | Pour / shake | "Now a refreshing drink" | Connect fruit to product |
| 4 | 15-22 sec | Product hero shot | Cup placed forward | "Fresh. Purple. Tropical." | Build memory |
| 5 | 23-30 sec | Store counter | Customer receives drink | "Try Mangosteen today" | CTA |

## 3. First Reaction Pillar

### Purpose
Use real reactions to create social proof and curiosity.

### Best For
- TikTok
- Reels
- Influencer content
- Staff tasting videos

### Story Structure
1. Introduce the taste test
2. Show first sip
3. Capture honest reaction
4. Describe taste in simple words
5. CTA

### Hook Examples
- "Trying our new Mangosteen drink for the first time."
- "Staff taste test: Mangosteen edition."
- "Would you try this purple drink?"

### Reaction Prompts
Ask the person tasting:
- "What does it taste like?"
- "Is it refreshing?"
- "Who would you recommend it to?"
- "Would you buy it again?"

### Text-on-Screen Examples
- "First sip reaction"
- "New Mangosteen launch"
- "Refreshing or not?"
- "Would you try this?"

### Example Storyboard

| Scene | Time | Visual | Action | Text on Screen | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0-2 sec | Staff holding drink | Looks at camera | "First sip of Mangosteen" | Hook |
| 2 | 3-6 sec | Close-up sip | Drinks | "Trying now..." | Build anticipation |
| 3 | 7-10 sec | Face reaction | Smile / surprise | "Oh, that's refreshing" | Social proof |
| 4 | 11-14 sec | Product close-up | Cup to camera | "Tropical + sweet + fresh" | Taste cue |
| 5 | 15 sec | Store / product shot | Point to counter | "Try it today" | CTA |

### Text-on-Screen Examples

- "Now available"
- "Try before it's gone"
- "New this month"

|---|---:|---|---|---|---|

| 2 | 3-5 sec | Staff serving | Hand over drink | "Mangosteen is here" | Availability |

## 5. In-Store Discovery Pillar

- "New drink spotted at our store."

### Text-on-Screen Examples

- "Available now"

| Scene | Time | Visual | Action | Text on Screen | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|

## 6. Staff Recommendation Pillar

### Purpose
Make the launch feel human and increase staff confidence in recommending it.

### Best For
- Staff training content
- Social content using real team members
- Store-level posts

### Story Structure
1. Staff introduces themselves
2. Staff introduces Mangosteen
3. Staff explains taste simply
4. Staff recommends who should try it
5. CTA

### Staff Script Template
"Hi, I'm [name] from [store]. Today we have a new Mangosteen drink. It tastes [simple taste description], and it is perfect for customers who want something [refreshing / tropical / not too heavy]. Try it today at [store]."

### Hook Examples
- "Our staff pick of the week: Mangosteen."
- "What our team recommends today."
- "New drink recommendation from our store."

### Example Storyboard

| Scene | Time | Visual | Action | Text on Screen | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0-3 sec | Staff smiles at camera | Holds product | "Staff pick: Mangosteen" | Human hook |
| 2 | 4-8 sec | Staff speaks | Explains taste | "Fresh + tropical" | Taste cue |
| 3 | 9-13 sec | Product close-up | Staff points to drink | "Perfect for hot days" | Use case |
| 4 | 14-18 sec | Counter handoff | Staff gives drink | "Available now" | Availability |
| 5 | 19-20 sec | Product hero shot | Logo / store shot | "Try it today" | CTA |

# Simple Scoring Rubric

Use this before approving a storyboard.

| Criteria | Score 1 | Score 3 | Score 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook strength | Weak opening | Clear opening | Very scroll-stopping |
| Product visibility | Product unclear | Product shown | Product is memorable |
| Content pillar match | Mixed / unclear | Mostly matches | Strongly matches one pillar |
| Message clarity | Confusing | Understandable | Simple and memorable |
| Filming practicality | Hard to shoot | Manageable | Easy for staff to film |
| CTA | Missing | Present | Clear and direct |

Minimum approval score: 24 out of 30.
