# Mangosteen Launch Marketing Plan

## Campaign Name
Mangosteen Launch Awareness Campaign

## Key Objective
Build strong market awareness for the Mangosteen launch by making target customers familiar with the product, brand story, taste profile, and launch availability.

## Primary Goal
Create broad awareness before and during launch so that customers recognize Mangosteen as a fresh, premium, and exciting new product.

## Target Audience

### Primary Audience
- Health-conscious consumers
- Juice, smoothie, and fresh drink buyers
- Young professionals and office workers
- Families looking for refreshing fruit-based drinks
- Existing customers of the brand's food and beverage outlets

### Secondary Audience
- Social media followers interested in new drinks and seasonal products
- Influencers and food reviewers
- Mall, airport, and high-footfall location customers

## Positioning
Mangosteen should be positioned as a refreshing tropical launch product with a premium Thai fruit identity. The campaign should highlight freshness, uniqueness, natural flavor, and seasonal excitement.

## Key Message
"Discover the refreshing taste of Mangosteen — a premium tropical flavor made to brighten your day."

## Supporting Messages
- Fresh tropical taste
- Premium fruit-inspired drink
- Limited launch / seasonal excitement
- Perfect refreshment for hot days
- A new flavor worth trying and sharing

## Campaign Strategy

### 1. Pre-Launch Awareness
Objective: Build curiosity before the product is available.

Actions:
- Teaser posts using mangosteen visuals, purple color cues, and close-up product shots
- Countdown posts: 7 days, 3 days, 1 day before launch
- Short teaser captions such as "A tropical favorite is coming soon"
- In-store teaser signage at key locations
- Staff preview and product education so frontline teams can create curiosity

### 2. Launch Week Push
Objective: Maximize visibility during the first week.

Actions:
- Announce the product across Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LINE OA, and in-store signage
- Use short-form video showing product preparation, first sip reactions, and fruit visuals
- Place launch posters near counters and queue areas
- Encourage staff to mention the new product to every relevant customer
- Offer a simple trial incentive such as add-on discount, combo pairing, or first-week launch price if margin allows

### 3. Social Media Content Plan
Objective: Make Mangosteen recognizable and shareable.

Content pillars:
- Product beauty shots
- Taste explanation
- Behind-the-scenes preparation
- Customer reactions
- Thai tropical fruit story
- Limited-time launch reminder

Suggested content:
- 3 launch announcement posts
- 5 to 7 short videos or reels
- Daily stories during launch week
- Influencer tasting content
- User-generated content reposts

### 4. Influencer and KOL Activation
Objective: Create third-party credibility and reach.

Approach:
- Invite micro-influencers in food, lifestyle, cafe, and family segments
- Focus on authentic tasting reactions rather than overly scripted reviews
- Prioritize influencers near launch store locations
- Ask for short videos, stories, and one clear call-to-action: "Try the new Mangosteen drink now"

### 5. In-Store Awareness
Objective: Convert foot traffic into awareness and trial.

Actions:
- Counter cards
- Menu board highlight
- Staff recommendation script
- Product sampling during peak hours if operationally feasible
- Purple visual theme near ordering point
- QR code linking to campaign page or social post

## Channel Plan

| Channel | Role | Key Activity |
|---|---|---|
| Facebook | Mass awareness | Launch posts, promo announcement, product education |
| Instagram | Visual desire | Reels, stories, product shots, influencer reposts |
| TikTok | Discovery | Short videos, first sip reaction, preparation clips |
| LINE OA | Existing customer push | Launch broadcast, coupon, store availability |
| In-store | Final conversion | Posters, staff recommendation, sampling |
| Influencers | Trust and reach | Review content and store visit posts |

## Suggested Timeline

### Week -2: Preparation
- Finalize product name, visuals, pricing, and store availability
- Produce photo and video assets
- Prepare store signage and staff briefing

### Week -1: Teaser
- Begin social media teasers
- Install in-store teaser materials
- Brief store teams
- Confirm influencer schedule

### Launch Week
- Full launch announcement
- Daily social content
- Influencer posts go live
- LINE OA broadcast
- In-store visibility and staff recommendation

### Week +1 to +3: Sustain Awareness
- Share customer reactions
- Repost influencer and customer content
- Remind customers that Mangosteen is new / limited / seasonal
- Track performance and optimize content

## KPI Framework

### Awareness KPIs
- Social reach
- Impressions
- Video views
- Engagement rate
- Follower growth
- Influencer reach
- In-store customer awareness feedback

### Trial Support KPIs
- Number of Mangosteen units sold
- Launch week sales by store
- Coupon redemption if used
- Sampling-to-purchase conversion if sampling is done

## Risks and Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Low customer understanding of product | Use simple taste description and strong visuals |
| Weak first-week visibility | Increase in-store signage and staff recommendation |
| Content does not generate engagement | Use video, reactions, and influencer content |
| Product availability issues | Launch in selected stores first and clearly communicate availability |
